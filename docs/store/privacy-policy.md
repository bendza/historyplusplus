# history++ privacy policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

history++ is a browser extension that keeps your browsing history on your own computer and lets you search it. This policy explains what it handles and where that data goes. In short: everything stays on your device.

## What history++ stores
- **Browsing history:** the address, title and time of the pages you visit, and how long you stay on them.
- **Page text:** the readable text of pages you stay on for at least a few seconds, so you can search inside them.
- **Search index:** data computed from the above so that search works, including "meaning" fingerprints of pages.
- **Settings:** your preferences, such as sites you choose never to record.

history++ does not record:
- pages in private (incognito) windows
- browser-internal pages
- pages on your local network
- a built-in list of well-known banking, payment, email, sign-in and health sites (no list can include every site, so add any others to your "never record" list)
- the text of any page with a password, one-time-code or payment-card field, including card fields hosted by payment providers
- sites you add to the "never record" list

From version 1.0.1, secret parts of addresses, such as password-reset and sign-in tokens or a user name and password in the address, are removed before an address is saved.

## Where it is stored
All of this data is stored only on your computer, inside the browser's private storage for the extension. history++ has no servers and no account system. It does not send your history, page text, searches, or any other personal data to its developer or to anyone else.

## Network access
history++ makes one kind of network request: a one-time download of the language model used for search by meaning, from huggingface.co. It happens only when you choose to turn on that feature. The request downloads model files to your computer. It contains none of your data.

## Backups and exports
If daily backup is on (the default), history++ saves a copy of its database to the Downloads folder on your computer. Exports you start yourself are also saved on your computer. These files contain your browsing history and are not encrypted; keep them as private as you would your browser profile. If your Downloads folder is synced by a cloud service (for example OneDrive, iCloud Drive or Dropbox), that service will copy these backups too. You can turn daily backup off in Settings.

## Sharing and selling
history++ does not sell, share, or transfer your data, and does not use it for advertising, credit decisions, or any purpose other than letting you search your own history.

## Your control
- You can pause recording, delete single pages, forget whole sites, or delete everything from Settings. From version 1.0.1, deleted data is also overwritten inside the database file. Backups made before a deletion still contain it until you delete them.
- Uninstalling the extension deletes its stored data. Backups in your Downloads folder remain until you delete them.
